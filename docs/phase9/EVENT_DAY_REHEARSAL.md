# Event-day rehearsal — go/no-go runbook

Use a disposable rehearsal event or data you are prepared to preserve in audit history. Never rehearse destructive corrections against real completed event records just to make the checklist green.

## Hardware matrix
Use at minimum: one captain phone, one facilitator phone, one second participant phone for Agent Contact if enabled, and the organizer laptop/phone. Use the same browsers and network paths expected onsite. Print at least one real station sign at event-day size.

## Rehearsal sequence
1. Open **Rehearsal** as organizer on the production HTTPS origin. Test server and camera. Confirm the browser reports a secure context.
2. Sign in on every real device. New accounts must have completed their forced password change.
3. Scan a printed station QR from the captain phone. Repeat with the manual code. Reject any sign that is slow or unreliable to acquire under venue lighting.
4. Run a complete station lifecycle on the facilitator phone: arrival/admit, start, finish, result review and confirmation. Confirm the captain immediately sees the canonical next mission/score after refresh/reconnect.
5. If Agent Contact is enabled, use two individual accounts. Scan the expiring QR, verify identity before confirmation, complete any recipient consent, then repeat with numeric fallback. A duplicate must not create extra award credit.
6. During a pending action, disable network on one device. Restore it and use the provided retry/recovery path. Verify that the final server record exists exactly once.
7. Send two or more teams toward the same queue-enabled station. Verify capacity and FIFO admission, and confirm a late refresh does not reorder already-admitted teams.
8. Pause and resume once. Confirm participant/facilitator screens communicate the state clearly. Exercise only the organizer overrides you expect staff to understand onsite.
9. End the rehearsal. Compare Mission Control and Results totals. Finalize and view the reveal from a participant account. Reopen, confirm the reveal is hidden, then finalize again and verify a new immutable revision is created.
10. Record device/browser/network failures outside the app. The local Rehearsal checklist is a convenience, not centralized certification evidence.

## Go criteria
GO only when the real phones can acquire printed QR codes consistently, numeric fallback works, reconnect does not duplicate mutations, simultaneous teams preserve queue/capacity rules, and the organizer can explain pause/end/reopen/results without developer assistance.

NO-GO if camera permission/HTTPS is broken, venue connectivity causes unrecoverable writes, any duplicate score/contact appears after retry, or staff cannot distinguish pending review from a confirmed result.
