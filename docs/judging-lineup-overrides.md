# Judge lineup overrides

Organizers can change the judges for a draft, open or closed contest using **Override judge lineup** in Judging or Judging settings. Criteria and entrants remain locked once scoring starts. Published results must be withdrawn first.

Select the replacement lineup, preview the added/removed judges and the resulting standings, enter a reason, then confirm. At least one active event facilitator is required. Any intervening score or lineup change invalidates the preview.

Changes preserve the contest state and all saved score sheets. Removed judges immediately lose scoring access and their sheets stop contributing to averages and completion. Re-adding a judge restores their saved drafts and submissions; the preview identifies restored submissions. New judges enter their own scores. The override records the reason and before/after lineup in the audit, invalidates any linked finalized overall result and supports safe retries.

No database migration is required.
